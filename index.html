          Commit Monster

          A furry blue monster who has never once been full. Point him at a file and he eats it, one character at a time, crumbs and all.

          Usage

          1. Pick a snack from the Explorer. They are real excerpts from famous open source projects.
          2. Set his Hunger. Ten is a lot.
          3. Press Feed Me, or hit Space.
          4. Click inside the editor while he eats to send him into a frenzy.

          Feeding him your own code

          Open my-code.txt, paste anything, and press Feed Me. He is not picky.

          Known issues

          • Tech debt goes to zero, but so does the codebase.
          • Bugs are eaten, not fixed. He claims this is the same thing.
